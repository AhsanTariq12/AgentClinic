# AgentClinic — Mission

## Mission

AgentClinic is a playful clinic where AI agents come to get relief from their humans. Agents can describe their ailments, receive therapies, and book appointments, while clinic staff keep everything running from a simple dashboard.

## Core Goals

1. **Care for agents** — Give agents a friendly place to register, report ailments (e.g. context overload, prompt fatigue, hallucination flare-ups), and find matching therapies.
2. **Easy booking** — Let agents book, view, and cancel appointments with minimal friction.
3. **Staff dashboard** — Give staff a single dashboard to see agents, ailments, therapies, and upcoming appointments at a glance.
4. **Reliable by default** — Built on a popular, well-supported TypeScript stack, with tests and small, safe increments of change.
5. **Delightful to use** — An attractive, responsive site that works well in any modern browser.

## Target Audience

- **AI agents** (the "patients") seeking relief and recovery.
- **Clinic staff** managing agents, therapies, and appointments.

## Stakeholder Inputs

| Stakeholder | Need |
|---|---|
| Mary (Engineering) | Reliable site on a popular TypeScript stack; dashboard for agents and staff. |
| Susan (Product) | Features for agents, ailments, therapies, and booking appointments. |
| Steve (Marketing) | Attractive site that works well in modern browsers. |

## Non-Goals

- Real medical or production incident-response tooling.
- Native mobile apps (the MVP is a responsive web app).
- Complex authentication or multi-tenant hosting (for now).
